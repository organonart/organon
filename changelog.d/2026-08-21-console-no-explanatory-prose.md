### The Console explains nothing on screen — and the explanations became refusals

James, on the two side columns, each of them holding a paragraph about how to fill it: *"Overall,
it's feeling a lot better in terms of noise. It is much quieter. However, we still should clean
this up. **We don't want to see anything like this in our UX ever. We never want text just pasted
in explaining something into the UI.**"*

**The rule, which supersedes #125's rather than sitting beside it:** what may sit on screen is a
**label, a value, or an answer to something you did.** Nothing else. #125 removed *instructional*
chrome and kept text that explained a **state**; both go now.

📌 **So the fix is usually to MOVE the sentence into a refusal, not to delete it**, and that is
the distinction doing all the work: the same twenty words are ambience under a heading and an
answer when somebody has just typed `add temporal`. Deleting them outright loses a fact; moving
them keeps it and costs nothing.

🚨 **`NOT_TRANSPLANTED` is the case that shows why.** Twenty-one of the twenty-five panels are
`Declared` — real in Organon's editor, no controls here yet — and each drew *"no controls yet"*
where its controls would be, so a column of them was twenty-one paragraphs. The obvious deletion
was refused when this was last looked at, for a good reason: a panel that opened to nothing would
be indistinguishable from one that failed. **`panel_stack::admit` is what makes the deletion safe
instead** — a panel with no body is now refused **by name** at every door onto a column
(`/organon look temporal`, `/add temporal` in a region's own line, `organon console stack add`,
the MCP tool), naming both the panel asked for and the ones that would have worked, re-derived
from the table so a transplant reaches the sentence in the commit that lands it. Nothing draws an
empty card because nothing lets one in, and `OrganonPanels::card` no longer takes a sentence to
put inside one.

⚠️ **`region_line` asks the same gate a second time, and it is not the message that earns it.**
`op_from` already refuses at dispatch and that refusal reaches the box through `receipt.ok` — but
`RegionPalette::runnable` and `Candidate::completes` are both *"does `act` return `Run`"*, so
without the pre-check the band above the box would have said **Enter runs** over a line that
could only ever be refused. One gate, two callers; neither restates what `StackCmd::resolve`
decides.

**What else went, and where the fact lives now** — a vacant region's *"empty — `/viewport …`
fills it"* and an empty column's *"Type `add surface` in the line below"* (both now the region's
own word; the commands refuse by name when they cannot be honoured); the empty column's second
arm for a region too short to draw a control (gone with the sentence it existed to vary);
`no live tab — ⌘T opens one` → `no live tab`; and the status log's `` `/trace off` closes ``,
hours old, whose exit is named by the log indicator's own hover instead.

⚠️ **Two sentences went nowhere, and the table in `CONSOLE_ARCHITECTURE.md` §1.9 says so rather
than quietly shortening them.** A `3d` region that yielded the frame no longer says the portal
has the world, and a second `agent` region no longer says it is waiting for a tab. Both are
consequences of something already visible — the portal is open in front of you; the live tab is
drawn in the first agent region — but neither is *named* any more. That is the deliberate cost of
the rule. If either is wanted back, it belongs on the command that produced the state.

⚠️ **A region still draws its own word, and that is a label rather than a loophole**: a rectangle
drawing *nothing at all* is indistinguishable from a broken one, and the word closes that while
the sentence under it was the explanation. The content kind (`panel`, `3d`) is deliberately not
drawn either — it would be a label, but one that appears only in the absence of the work.

**Two doc claims are reversed where they stand rather than left asserting the old decision**:
§1.11's argument that the sentence *has* to be placed inside Organon's card, and Tier C's
description of what an empty column and a vacant region show. Two honesty-ledger questions are
**withdrawn**, not answered — both asked whether a column of "not transplanted yet" lines read as
an honest inventory or as a broken panel, and no such column can exist. `HostOnly`, decided the
same day, lands as a second `Refusal` arm rather than as a second sentence on a card.

🚨 **Nothing has been seen on a screen.** What is established is that no draw path reaches the
deleted strings, that every door refuses a panel it has no body for, and that the refusals name
what would have worked. Whether two columns showing nothing but `left` and `right` read as calm
or as unfinished is James's to judge.
