### Three CouchStation patterns cut in the Organon Mind template, to find out whether the form holds for hardware

`doc/couchstation.md` §5 carries fifteen patterns in Alexander's form, and they are all
about **where objects go**. Reading the rest of the capture against the Organon Mind
catalogue surfaces three more languages the document contains and never names: a
**control** language in §7, a **form** language in §8, and a **method** language across
§3, §4.2 and §9. `doc/couchstation-patterns-draft.md` cuts one entry from each into the
template the published catalogue uses, as a test of the form rather than as a section of
a specification.

📌 **The template needed one addition and it is not optional here.** Every entry carries
an `Evidence` field opening with a ledger marker. OM-004 states its evidence position
once for the whole paper, because the known use of all fourteen entries there is one
workstation in one room. That will not transfer: these three sit at three different tiers
of support inside a document whose headline fact is that nothing in it is measured, so an
entry without its own marker would inherit the confidence of its neighbours. The Held
Mode is *Untested*; the other two are *Argued*, one of them for a procedure nobody has
carried out yet. Each says so in its own field.

⚠️ **`Structure` is the field that strains, and it is worth watching before a fourth
entry.** In the software catalogue it carries a sequence diagram or a state branch and
the figure does real work. Two of these three want an orthographic sketch or a
dimensioned photograph instead, and the method entry has no physical structure at all —
its Structure is a table of a process, which is the field being used for something the
template did not anticipate.

One field went the other way. *How you would know this is unnecessary* is the hardest
field in the software catalogue and was the easiest here, because a hardware pattern's
retirement condition is usually a device that does not exist yet, and naming that device
is concrete. Two of the three name one. The third retires itself: the fitting flow §4.2
argues for would remove Tier 1's reason to exist, and the check is whether a derived
position and a discovered one agree for the same body.

Nothing was promoted a tier and no dimension was invented. The Measured column of
`couchstation.md` §1 is still empty, and the draft's closing section records the two open
naming questions rather than settling them quietly.
